# When to Self-Serve vs Contact HR (Acme HK — Demo)

**Document type:** Operating guide for employees and People Ops  
**Purpose:** Relieve HR of repetitive Tier-1 questions while protecting escalations that need humans

---

## 1. Self-serve first (use HR Knowledge Assistant / People Portal)

Use the assistant or FAQ when you need:

- Standard **policy explanations** (leave entitlement rules, hybrid pattern, expense thresholds)  
- **How-to steps** in SuccessFactors (request leave, update address, submit expenses)  
- Links to the right form or module name  
- “What documents do I need?” for standard cases  

**Success look like:** employee completes the transaction or understands the rule without an HR ticket.

## 2. Contact HR / HRBP (open a ticket)

| Situation | Why human |
|-----------|-----------|
| Exception to policy (extra leave, unpaid leave, remote abroad >5 days) | Discretion + risk |
| Payroll discrepancy / wrong pay | Sensitive + audit |
| Harassment, grievance, performance dispute | Confidential process |
| Medical long-term absence / disability accommodation | Case management |
| Immigration / employment letter / visa | Controlled documents |
| System says you lack permission after following how-to | Access / data fix |
| You disagree with a declined leave and need mediation | Judgment |

## 3. How to open an HR ticket (demo process)

1. People Portal → **HR Service Desk** / **Ask HR**.  
2. Choose category (Pay, Leave exception, Data correction, Other).  
3. Describe the issue **without** pasting full HKID/bank numbers.  
4. Attach screenshots with sensitive digits masked.  
5. Submit; default SLA **2 business days** for Tier-1, longer for complex cases.

## 4. What HR should not be asked first

- “How do I click request time off?” → how-to doc  
- “How many AL days is the standard entitlement?” → leave policy  
- “What’s the hybrid default?” → hybrid policy  
- “How do I change my phone number?” → personal info how-to  

These are **deflection targets** for the Track B assistant.

## 5. Metrics People Ops cares about (portfolio signal)

| Metric | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| % Tier-1 deflected | Lower ticket volume on standard Qs |
| Citation rate | Answers grounded in policy pack |
| Escalation accuracy | Hard cases still reach humans fast |
| CSAT on self-serve | Employees trust the answers |

## 6. AI assistant hard rules

1. Answer from **policy + how-to** with citations.  
2. If the user needs an **exception, pay fix, or sensitive case**, tell them to **open HR ticket** and stop inventing approvals.  
3. Never pretend to have submitted a workflow.  
4. Never reveal other employees’ data.  
5. Prefer short step lists employees can follow in SF.
