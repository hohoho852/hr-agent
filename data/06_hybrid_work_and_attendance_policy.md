# Hybrid Work and Attendance Policy (Acme HK — Demo)

**Document type:** Company policy  
**Applies to:** Office-based and hybrid employees (Hong Kong)

---

## 1. Default model

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Standard pattern | **3 days in office / 2 days remote** per week unless role is office-mandatory |
| Core collaboration hours | **10:00–16:00 HKT** — be reachable on Teams |
| Office days | Team-agreed; managers may set fixed anchor days (e.g. Tue/Wed/Thu) |
| Fully remote | Exception only; People Ops + department head approval |

## 2. Booking the office

1. Use the **Workplace / desk booking** tool linked from the People Portal.  
2. Book before 17:00 the prior working day when possible.  
3. Cancel unused desks so others can book.

## 3. Attendance expectations

- Be online and responsive during core hours on remote days.  
- Calendar should show focus time vs meetings honestly.  
- Repeated no-shows on agreed office days may trigger performance discussion.

## 4. Working from outside Hong Kong

Working abroad (including WFH while travelling) requires **manager + HR pre-approval** for tax, immigration, and data reasons. Tourist “work from beach” without approval is not allowed for periods over **5 consecutive working days**.

## 5. Overtime and after-hours

Non-managerial overtime follows the Employment contract and OT policy. Hybrid work is not a substitute for unpaid overtime expectations.

## 6. What the assistant may say

- Explain 3/2 default, core hours, desk booking pointer, overseas work rule.  
- Direct exceptions to manager/HR.  
- Must not grant remote-work exceptions.
