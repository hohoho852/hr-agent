# Personal Data and Employee Profile Policy (Demo HK)

**Document type:** Company policy + privacy summary  
**System:** Company HR system — Employee Profile / Personal Information  
**Related how-to:** `05_how_to_update_personal_info.md`

---

## 1. Purpose

Employees must keep personal data accurate so payroll, tax, benefits, and emergency response work correctly. This policy states **what you may change yourself**, **what HR must change**, and **privacy rules**.

## 2. Data you must keep current

| Data | Why it matters | Who updates |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Residential address | Tax, mail, emergency | Employee self-service (with effective date) |
| Personal mobile / email | Reachability | Employee |
| Emergency contact | Crisis response | Employee |
| Bank account for salary | Payroll | Employee self-service **or** HR if locked |
| Marital status / dependents | Benefits eligibility | Employee with evidence where required |
| Legal name / ID / passport | Legal / immigration | **HR only** with supporting documents |
| Job, grade, manager, pay | Employment terms | **HR / manager workflow only** |

## 3. Self-service vs HR ticket

**Use self-service** for standard contact and address changes.  
**Open an HR ticket** for:

- Legal name change (deed poll / marriage cert)  
- HKID / passport / visa updates  
- Correction of historical employment data  
- Suspected identity theft or wrong person data  
- Any change you cannot save (field locked)

## 4. Privacy and acceptable use

1. Do not share colleagues’ personal data in Slack/Teams/email beyond need-to-know.  
2. Do not upload other people’s ID documents into the HR assistant chat.  
3. AI tools may explain **how** to update fields; they must not store or repeat full bank account numbers or HKID in answers.  
4. Screenshots for support: mask account numbers except last 4 digits.  
5. Company may process employee data under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and the employee privacy notice.

## 5. Effective dating

Most biographic changes in the employee profile are **effective-dated**. Choose the date the change becomes true (e.g. move-in date). Back-dating beyond policy limits may require HR.

## 6. Verification

HR may request documents for high-impact changes (name, bank, dependents). Failure to provide documents can delay payroll or benefits.

## 7. What the assistant may say

- Explain which fields are self-service vs HR-only.  
- Link to the step-by-step update guide.  
- Remind users not to paste full sensitive IDs into chat.  
- **Must not** change profile data or confirm payroll bank updates succeeded without the employee checking the system.
