# Leave and Time Off Policy (Demo HK)

**Document type:** Company policy  
**Applies to:** Full-time employees on Demo Hong Kong Limited payroll  
**System:** Company HR system — Time Off  
**Related how-to:** `03_how_to_request_time_off.md`

---

## 1. Annual leave (AL)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Entitlement (default) | **15 working days** per leave year after probation confirmation |
| During probation | Accrues but normally **not taken** unless manager + HR approve |
| Leave year | 1 January – 31 December |
| Carry-over | Up to **5 unused days** into next year; must be used by **31 March** or forfeited |
| Half-day | Allowed (morning or afternoon) |
| Minimum notice | **3 working days** for ≤3 days; **10 working days** for >3 consecutive days |
| Peak / blackout | Last two weeks of December and major product release weeks may be restricted; manager may decline non-critical AL |

Unused AL above carry-over is **not paid out** except on termination as required by Hong Kong Employment Ordinance and company practice.

## 2. Sick leave (SL)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Paid sick leave | As per Hong Kong Employment Ordinance entitlements after qualifying service |
| Medical certificate | Required for **2 or more consecutive calendar days**, or any day adjacent to a public holiday / AL if requested by manager |
| Same-day notice | Notify manager **before start of shift** (or as soon as practicable) and log absence in Time Off within **24 hours** |
| Hospitalisation | Separate paid sick leave balance may apply; contact HR for long-term cases |

Do **not** use annual leave to hide illness if you are unwell and eligible for sick leave.

## 3. Other leave types (summary)

| Type | Summary |
|------|---------|
| Marriage leave | **3 working days** (once) with marriage certificate; take within 30 days of ceremony |
| Compassionate leave | **3 working days** for immediate family; manager + HR |
| Maternity / paternity | Statutory minimums + company top-up where stated in Benefits guide |
| Jury / civic duty | Unpaid or as required by law; notify HR with official notice |
| Unpaid leave | Exception only; HR + manager written approval |

## 4. Public holidays

Demo Hong Kong Limited observes the Hong Kong general holiday calendar published each year on the People Portal. Working on a public holiday requires pre-approval and TOIL or pay per contract.

## 5. Approval rules

1. Employee submits in **Time Off** in the company HR system.  
2. **Line manager** approves or declines.  
3. HR may override only for policy/compliance exceptions.  
4. Taking leave **without an approved request** may be treated as unauthorised absence.

## 6. Balance visibility

Employees can see projected balances in Time Off → **My Leave Requests / Balances**. Balances are estimates until payroll period close; disputes go to HR with screenshots — not via public chat.

## 7. What the assistant may say

- Explain entitlement rules, notice periods, carry-over, and required documents.  
- Point to the how-to for submitting a request.  
- **Must not** invent remaining balance numbers for a named person.  
- **Must not** approve leave or promise exceptions.
