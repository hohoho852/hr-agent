# How to Submit an Expense Claim (Employee Guide)

**Audience:** Employees  
**System:** Company HR / finance portal (demo assumes **Expenses** tile)  
**Policy anchors:** Receipts required; manager approval; finance audit

---

## Goal

Claim legitimate business expenses so you are reimbursed on the next eligible payroll or AP run.

## Policy summary (quick)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Receipts | Required for any item **≥ HKD 100**; always preferred |
| Claim window | Submit within **30 days** of spend |
| Personal spend | Not claimable |
| Alcohol / client entertainment | Pre-approval from manager; within entertainment guideline |
| Travel | Align to Travel policy; economy default under 6 hours flight |
| Approval | Line manager; Finance may spot-check |

## Steps

1. Sign in to the **People Portal** (company HR / finance tools).  
2. Open **Expenses** (or **My Expenses**).  
3. Click **Create expense report** / **New claim**.  
4. Enter **report name** (e.g. “Client visit – 12 Jul”).  
5. **Add expense line** for each item:
   - Date  
   - Category (meals, transport, lodging, other)  
   - Amount and currency  
   - Business purpose (one sentence)  
6. **Attach receipt** image/PDF per line (or header if tool allows).  
7. Add cost center / project code if prompted (ask your manager if unsure).  
8. **Submit** for approval.  
9. Track status under **My reports**.

## After submit

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Draft | Not submitted |
| Pending approval | With manager |
| Approved | In finance queue |
| Sent back | Fix comments and resubmit |
| Paid / Reimbursed | Funds processed — check bank statement |

## Mobile tips

- Photograph receipts **immediately**; glare-free, full page.  
- Do not crop away merchant name, date, or total.

## Common failures

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Missing receipt | Attach or provide card statement + explanation for <100 HKD only |
| Wrong category | Edit line and resubmit |
| Past 30 days | Request manager exception note; Finance may still reject |
| Duplicate claim | Withdraw duplicate |

## Guardrails for AI answers

- Teach the steps and thresholds.  
- Do not promise reimbursement dates beyond “next eligible cycle after approval”.  
- Do not approve claims.  
- Flag personal expenses as not claimable.
